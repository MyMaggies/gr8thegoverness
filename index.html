Play Summary
In Neil Simon's The Governess[cite: 32], a timid governess named Julia is called into her employer's study to settle her accounts. Julia is supposed to be paid 40 rubles per month. However, the Mistress acts in an authoritative manner [cite: 62], cruelly deducting money for unfair reasons to see if Julia will stand up for herself[cite: 80, 82]. Throughout the conversation, Julia remains frightened and keeps her head down, highlighting the harsh reality of social class differences[cite: 54, 55].
