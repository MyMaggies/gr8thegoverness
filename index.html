Play Summary
In Neil Simon's The Governess, a timid governess named Julia is called into her employer's study to settle her accounts. Julia is supposed to be paid 40 rubles per month[cite: 8, 10]. However, the Mistress acts in an authoritative manner[cite: 61, 62], cruelly deducting money for unfair reasons to see if Julia will stand up for herself. Throughout the conversation, Julia remains frightened [cite: 71, 73] and keeps her head down [cite: 41], highlighting the harsh reality of social class differences[cite: 36, 54].
