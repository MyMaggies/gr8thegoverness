Play Summary
In Neil Simon's The Governess, a timid governess named Julia is called into her employer's study to settle her accounts. Julia is supposed to be paid 40 rubles per month[cite: 10, 93]. However, the Mistress acts in an authoritative manner , cruelly deducting money for unfair reasons to see if Julia will stand up for herself[cite: 80, 82]. Throughout the conversation, Julia remains frightened and keeps her head down [cite: 41, 43, 73, 89], highlighting the harsh reality of social class differences[cite: 36, 54, 55].
